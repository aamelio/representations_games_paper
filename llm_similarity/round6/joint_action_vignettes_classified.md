# Joint-action vignettes with classifications

# DG

## DG-M-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Moral
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: None
* Joint action: C-NA

Anna receives a set of meal vouchers and must decide how many to keep and how many to give to Marco. She says that, since both of them have the same need for meals, the fair choice is to divide the vouchers equally.

## DG-M-K

* Setting: anonymous market
* Sender category: Moral
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: None
* Joint action: C-NA

Firm A received a transaction bonus from an online platform for completing an order with the platform. The manager of firm A splits the bonus with firm B, its input supplier.



## DG-S-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Self-interest
* Sender action: D
* Receiver action: None
* Joint action: D-NA

David receives some travel credits from a travel agency. He thinks whether to give some to his employee, Laura, but decides to keep them all to himself, as the credits were awarded to him and he values them quite a bit.

## DG-S-K

* Setting: anonymous market
* Sender category: Self-interest
* Sender action: D
* Receiver action: None
* Joint action: D-NA

An online marketplace assigns a sales rebate to a selling firm and allows it to transfer any part of it to the buyer. The seller's manager decides to keep all the rebate, to maximize the firm's earnings.

## DG-C-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Cooperation
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: None
* Joint action: C-NA

Giovanni receives the reward from a project he completed jointly with Paola and decides how much of it to give her. Because working together created much more value than either of them could have produced alone, Giovanni gives Paola a substantial share so that both can benefit from the returns generated by their joint work.


# UG

## UG-M-CC-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Moral
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: C-C

A local theater offers some concert credits to two friends, Elena and Paolo, who must jointly decide whether to accept the credits. Elena proposes a 50:50 split arguing it's the fair outcome. Paolo accepts the offer, so the obtain the credits.

## UG-M-CC-K

* Setting: anonymous market
* Sender category: Moral
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: C-C

An online platform gives a bonus to Firm a subject on A's input supplier, Firm B, accepting the giveaway. Firm A assesses that an equal split is fair, and B accepts it. The bonus is thus distributed to the firms.

## UG-M-CD-P

* Sender category: Moral

* Receiver action: D
* Joint action: C-D

## UG-S-DC-P

## UG-S-DC-K

* Setting: anonymous market
* Sender category: Self-interest
* Sender action: D
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: D-C

Firm A must set the price for its product for a final good producer, Firm B. The firm sets a high price , leaving little margin for B. Firm B accepts, because some margin is better than none.

## UG-C-CC-P

* Sender category: Cooperation

## UG-C-CC-K

* Sender category: Cooperation
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: C-C

Two firms can undertake a joint project, but first they need to agree on how to split future profits. One firm argues that this is a terrific opportunity for both, so proposes an equal split. The other firm accepts, and they undertake the project and split the profits.

# TG

## TG-M-CC-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Moral
* Sender action: C
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: C-C

Miriam has a savings account with some dollars and decides how many to send to Roberto. Roberto invests the dollars at a 300% return and decides how many to return to Miriam. Miriam says her savings account equally is fair and sends that to Roberto. Roberto returns a fair share of his investment output, so both people gain.

## TG-M-CC-K

* Setting: anonymous market
* Sender category: Moral
* Sender action: C
* Sender allocation: keeps 3, gives 3
* Receiver action: C
* Joint action: C-C

Firm A receives a payment from an online platform. It must now decide how much to send to Firm B, which can reinvest at a 300% return. Firm A says that sending half of the initial financing is fair. Firm B invests the money and returns a sizable share to Firm A, so both firms gain.

## TG-S-DC-P

* Setting: personal
* Sender category: Self-interest
* Sender action: D
* Sender allocation: sends little
* Receiver action: C
